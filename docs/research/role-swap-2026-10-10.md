# Controlled final role swap — 10 October 2026

This isolated pilot tests one eligible role-pair swap after a completed Standard draft. It preserves hero picks, opening randomness and the other three intended roles. Explicitly locked heroes are excluded from swaps. Production remains v2.7.7 pending evidence and integration validation.

## Completed result

The pilot had positive point estimates, but insufficient evidence for default activation. All sixteen planned pairs completed, with 256 held-out profiles per policy. Standard won 131 and had 71 severe losses; the controlled-swap candidate won 132 and had 66 severe losses. These are native simulator outcomes, not measured human PvP win rates.

| Metric | Controlled swap minus Standard | One-sided 95% bound | Frozen requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Simulator successes | +0.3906 percentage points | Lower: −0.7813 points | Lower ≥ −0.5 points: fail |
| Severe losses | −1.9531 points | Upper: +1.5625 points | Upper ≤ +0.5 points: fail |
| First-pick / first-ban entropy | 0 / 0 bits | Unchanged by design | Not this experiment's objective |

Only three drafts accepted a swap. Their held-out win changes were −2, +2 and +1 cases; severe-loss changes were +2, +1 and −8. Most of the severe-loss benefit came from one draft. Thirteen unchanged role states reused explicitly marked deterministic baseline optimization and evaluation. These zero-difference pairs remain observations in the paired estimate, not additional simulator executions.

Screening examined 25 eligible pairs and made 123 native simulation calls, including sixteen baseline screens. Nineteen executed optimizations examined 1,900 item candidates and made 12,939 optimizer simulator calls. Drafting and openings did not change. Independent Python recomputation matched totals, rate changes, paired profiles, two-role-only changes, reuse and every accepted swap's training inequalities. JavaScript also checked frozen seeds/strata, native-eligible unique roles, selected item legality and training-case exclusion.

Do not enable this as default Auto on the pilot alone. The point estimates make independent confirmation reasonable, but they do not establish a reliable improvement. No thresholds, candidate exposure or sample size were tuned after outcomes, and this pilot was not enlarged. A separate confirmation must use new drafts and a frozen protocol before any integration decision.

This differs from the rejected provisional-role policy, which recomputed intended roles throughout drafting and changed subsequent picks. Here, drafting has finished before a swap is considered. The proposal is inspired by examining individual swap benefits rather than releasing all assignments; it is not a learned professional-player model.

## Frozen policy and pilot

The [protocol](role-swap-protocol.json) was saved before testing. Sixteen fresh paired seeds `137000041 + index × 104729` cover both factions and four opponent styles. Each faction/opponent stratum contains only two draft pairs. No previous batch or uploaded match is confirmation data.

The baseline's native best draft-value strategy is held fixed during role screening. Baseline and each eligible pair receive the same three native enemy presets. Own items are native defaults for each tested role assignment. Every case must preserve simulated wins and signed gold, and at least one must improve gold strictly. One qualified swap is chosen by the existing wins-then-mean comparator; otherwise all intended roles are retained. At most ten pairs are screened, requiring at most 33 additional simulator calls including baseline. No recursive swaps or future-team rollouts are performed.

After role screening, both policies use the unchanged production strategy/item optimizer with frozen time and a 100-item-candidate budget. Changed roles affect defaults and item candidate ordering; this is a role-plus-role-dependent-build intervention, not an independent pure-role causal estimate. The other three role assignments are preserved, but final strategy and item optimization can still change the team configuration.

Each output receives sixteen identical paired enemy strategy/eligible-role/default-item-order profiles, excluding exact preset/default-opponent training cases. Enemy custom item identities and all role permutations remain unmodeled. An unchanged role state reuses marked deterministic baseline optimization/evaluation; execution totals exclude reuse. Ten thousand stratified paired whole-draft bootstrap samples treat drafts, rather than individual profiles, as sampling units. Success lower one-sided 95% bound must be at least −0.5 percentage points; severe-loss upper bound must be at most +0.5 points. Severe loss means lose with signed final gold below −20,000. Opening diversity is unchanged and is not this final-configuration experiment's objective.

A small pilot cannot certify human PvP gains or live timer/control integration. Its training advantages need not transfer to held-out settings. No thresholds are tuned on this batch, and passing permits further validation rather than automatic activation.

Regression tests exercise eligible pair-only changes, explicit locks, per-scenario qualification, ties/native fallback, correct role defaults and immutable inputs. The manual source check at 15:54 Jakarta on 10 October found the 50 inspected public draft/Daily assets unchanged; private-server parity remains unverified.
